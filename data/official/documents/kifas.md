Minimum Co-payment
The minimum co-payment amount is the minimum amount of programme fees that you are required to pay after KiFAS subsidies. The amount increases progressively as your household or per capita income rises.
Example
If your child is an SC child in the kindergarten programme at an AOP kindergarten and your family’s gross monthly household income is $3,000 and below, you are eligible for a maximum KiFAS subsidy of $163 with a minimum co-payment of $1. However, if your child’s monthly programme fee is $160, the actual amount of KiFAS subsidy that you will receive will be $159 based on the $1 minimum co-payment applicable for your household income tier.
Refer to the KiFAS subsidy tables to find out the amount of KiFAS subsidy your child may be eligible for based on your HHI or PCI. The amount shown is an estimate. The actual subsidy will be determined based on verified details, such as income.

How to apply for KiFAS subsidy
The kindergarten will assist you with the KiFAS application at the point of your child’s enrolment. You may also apply for a KiFAS reassessment if there is a change in your family’s circumstances during your child’s enrolment at the kindergarten.
