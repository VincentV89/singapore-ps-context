## Verbatim eligibility or applicant scope excerpt

## Verbatim support excerpt

Types of SkillsFuture Credit 1. Opening SkillsFuture Credit — $500 This base tier credit can be used on top of existing SkillsFuture course fee subsidies to pay for a wide range of approved skills-related courses. It has no expiry date and is provided to all Singapore Citizens aged 25 and above. All courses listed on MySkillsFuture portal are eligible for SkillsFuture Credit (Opening Credit). They include: Courses subsidised or approved by the Skills and Workforce Development Agency (SWDA), Selected courses offered by Ministry of Education-funded institutions such as Institute of Technical Education, polytechnics, autonomous universities, LASALLE College of the Arts and Nanyang Academy of Fine Arts, Courses supported by lead agencies, and SkillsFuture@PA courses offered by the People’s Association and selected courses under the National Silver Academy. 2. SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career) — $4,000 This credit supports Singapore Citizens aged 40 and above. It can be used to offset out-of-pocket course fees for selected courses that have better employability outcomes, and it does not expire. Eligible courses include: Institute of Higher Learning (IHL) full qualifications and shorter modules that stack to a full qualification, MOE-subsidised full qualifications from the University of the Arts Singapore and Arts Institutions, SkillsFuture Career Transition Programmes (SCTP), which are train-and-place programmes in sectors with good hiring opportunities, Courses aligned to training requirements in Progressive Wage Model (PWM) sectors, and Workplace Literacy and Numeracy courses. Note : Any balance from the one-off Additional SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career Support) , which was provided to every Singapore Citizen aged 40 to 60 (inclusive) as at 31 December 2020, is now merged with the SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career) and no longer carries an expiry date. Top FAQs For SkillsFuture Credit
What can I use my SkillsFuture Credit for?
You can use your SkillsFuture Credit (Base Tier) for all courses listed on MySkillsFuture portal.
SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career) can only be used for selected courses. The course directory and detail page for these courses will display the relevant label.
SkillsFuture Credit can be used on top of other government course subsidies.
How do I submit a claim for SkillsFuture Credit?
Please refer to the guide
here
.
Can I use my SkillsFuture Credit (Base Tier) for a course that is not listed on MySkillsFuture portal? How can I do so?
If you have identified a good course that supports your employment but it is not found on MySkillsFuture portal, you could send your request to us via our
service portal
. We will evaluate your request and inform you of the outcome within 10 working days.
We will only review requests to use SkillsFuture Credit (Base Tier) for course(s) completed not more than 6 months from the date of receipt of request.
Can I use my SkillsFuture (Mid-Career) for a course that is not listed on MySkillsFuture portal?
You can only use your SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career) for courses that fall into the following categories:
1. Institutes of Higher Learning (IHL) full qualifications and shorter modules that stack to a full qualification,
2. MOE-subsidised full qualifications from the University of the Arts Singapore and Arts Institutions,
3. SkillsFuture Career Transition Programme (SCTP), which are train-and-place programmes in sectors with good hiring opportunities,
4. Courses aligned to training requirements in Progressive Wage Model (PWM) sectors, and
5. Workplace Literacy and Numeracy courses.
