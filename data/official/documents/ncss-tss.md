## Verbatim support excerpt

Grant quantum Funding is available to SSAs across 3 components, at up to 80% co-funding and up to 3-years. Funding under this scheme is assessed on the merits of proposals. Proposals will be assessed on factors such as the agency's alignment to social service delivery, the relevance of the capability being built, the agency's need for funding support, the cost-effectiveness of the proposed approach, and the expected benefit to the sector. Part A – Consultancy and Project implementation support Funding of up to $250,000, capped at $200,000 for headcount support. Example: Engage consultants to conduct compensation and benefits review or develop and implement volunteer management framework. Fund project headcount to lead transformation projects. Part B - Bespoke digitalisation projects Funding of up to $150,000. Examples: Custom web portals for service delivery, AI chatbots for automated responses, or therapeutic robotics solutions. Part C – Pre-scoped and green-laned solutions Funding of up to $150,000 in total. Up to $40,000 per solution or refer to specific Green Lane solutions' funding cap. Examples: HR management systems, account management systems, event booking platforms, CRM systems, or API integrations. Agencies may refer to the list of pre-scoped and green lane solutions , e.g. digital systems, cybersecurity data protection consultancy, for more information.

Overview
The Transformation Sustainability Scheme (TSS) provides funding to social service agencies (SSA) for consultancy, digital projects, and project implementation support.
The scheme focuses on strengthening seven critical organisational health areas: Strategy & Leadership, Governance, People, Finances, Digitalisation, Communications & Partnerships, and User-Centric Services.
Note: Previously funded through the Tech-and-GO! scheme
