The Capability Development Grant (CD Grant) encourages and supports the continuous professional development of the people that work in Singapore’s arts and culture sector.
The grant is open to individuals and organisations and supports the following training activities or programmes that span a duration of no more than 12 consecutive months:
Professional training and skills development programmes
On-the-job training activities
