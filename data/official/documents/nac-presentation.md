## Verbatim eligibility or applicant scope excerpt

## Verbatim support excerpt

The National Arts Council (NAC)’s Presentation and Participation (P&P) Grant aims to enable a wide range of arts practitioners and organisations to express and enrich our artistic diversity, as well as increase arts appreciation and participation in Singapore.

## Verbatim programme lifecycle update

From 16 April 2026 onwards , the Production Grant and Presentation & Participation (Extended Play) grant, which were previously open for application annually, can now be applied under the Presentation & Participation (General) grant. In order to accommodate projects under both the Production Grant and Presentation & Participation (Extended Play) Grant, the grant quantum cap for the Presentation & Participation (General) Grant will now be raised to $100,000 per applicant per Financial Year.
